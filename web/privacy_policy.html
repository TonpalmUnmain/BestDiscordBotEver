BestDiscordBotEver — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2025-11-04

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information the BestDiscordBotEver bot (the “Bot”), operated by TonpalmUnmain, may collect when used and how that information is used, stored, and disclosed.

2. Data We Collect

  • Discord-provided identifiers and metadata (user ID, username, display name, roles) necessary for bot functionality.
  • Message text and content when messages are processed for moderation, commands, logging, or features (for example, message logging, banned-word detection).
  • Optional files or attachments explicitly uploaded to the bot via file commands.
  • Operational logs (timestamps, command usage, errors) for debugging and maintenance.

3. How We Use Data

Data is used to provide bot features: respond to commands, manage users, moderate content, operate voice and file features, and to maintain logs for stability and abuse prevention.

4. Storage & Retention

Data is stored locally on the host running the bot in project folders (e.g., userdata/, fdump/, log/). Retention duration is configurable; logs and user snapshots may be kept until rotated or deleted by the operator.

5. Sharing & Third Parties

The Bot does not sell personal data. The operator may share data with third-party services only when necessary for functionality (e.g., yt-dlp/stream resolution services, FFmpeg execution). Any third-party service use is subject to those providers’ policies.

6. Security

Reasonable administrative and technical measures should be used by the bot operator to protect stored data. The Bot stores data on the operator’s host; security depends on the operator’s environment and configuration.

7. User Controls & Requests

Users may request deletion or correction of stored user-specific data by contacting the bot operator. The Bot provides admin commands to manage user data and files; the operator is responsible for honoring requests.

8. Children

The Bot is not intended for children under 13. The operator will not knowingly collect data from children under applicable age limits.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated. Changes will be reflected in this document with an updated effective date.

10. Contact

For questions or data requests, contact the bot operator: TonpalmUnmain. Provide request details and a user identifier (Discord ID) when applicable.

Project: BestDiscordBotEver — Local bot. Data storage and administration are controlled by the operator hosting the bot.
